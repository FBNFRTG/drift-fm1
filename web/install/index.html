Install DRIFT.

FM-1 · Version 0.9.1 · Identity FM-1_983

  1. Back up your patches and samples.
  2. Connect your FM-1 with a USB data cable. Close other MIDI apps and the sample uploader.
  3. Use Chrome or Edge, allow MIDI access, and keep this page open throughout installation.

The installer checks the bundled firmware file and the device model before starting. Do not disconnect USB or power during the write. An interrupted update can be resumed by reconnecting and pressing Install again.

This custom firmware and its installer have not been tested here on a physical FM-1. The three sample slots remain compatible with DRIFT 0.3.
